# Product

## Register

product

## Users

IT technicians at techbold managing service desk tickets. Working in a focused, task-oriented flow: pick up an assigned incident, investigate a customer's Linux VM, apply a diagnosed fix (AI-proposed, human-approved), validate the outcome, document the resolution. The workspace is the whole job — open for the duration of every active incident.

## Product Purpose

An AI-assisted service desk autopilot for techbold technicians. Reads tickets from the Phoenix ERP, connects to the affected customer VM via SSH, runs an AI-powered diagnostic loop where the technician approves every proposed command, validates the fix, and submits a structured activity log back to the ERP. The AI reduces cognitive load and time-to-resolution; the human retains full control. Success: fewer escalations, faster resolution, zero undocumented incidents.

## Brand Personality

Professional, dependable, sharp. Techbold's own voice — "die Extrameile ist unser Standard" (the extra mile is the standard). The tool should feel built by techbold for techbold: on-brand, serious, no-nonsense. Three words: precise, trustworthy, efficient.

## Anti-references

- Consumer chat interfaces (Intercom, ChatGPT web UI) — too casual, wrong register
- Hacker / terminal aesthetic (black background, green text, retro monospace) — cliché and off-brand for a corporate IT firm
- Generic SaaS admin dashboards (light gray everything, no brand, forgettable) — invisible
- Dark mode with purple / neon gradients — wrong tone for a professional IT service company

## Design Principles

1. **Human control is visible.** Every AI-proposed action has an explicit approve / reject, never buried. The human is always clearly in the loop.
2. **Incidents are the center.** The workspace centers the ticket and customer context, not the chat or the AI. The tool serves the work.
3. **On techbold's brand.** Navy + gold color system, professional typography. A technician opening this should see a natural extension of techbold's identity.
4. **Signal over noise.** Critical info (ticket status, customer system, AI confidence) is immediately readable. Secondary info is one step away.
5. **Audit is always present.** Every action is timestamped and logged. The technician sees what happened; the activity writes itself from that log.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Navy/white pairings must clear 4.5:1. Gold (#fcb514) fails on white as text — use only as a background color paired with dark text, or as a decorative accent. Reduced motion required for all state transition animations.
